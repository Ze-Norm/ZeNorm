# ZeNorm integration

## Records and scope

Use the supplied ZeNorm tools for records, claims, dependencies and validated mutations. Services own persistence, revisions, locks and scheduling. In a direct repository session, the repository's own planning records under `.scratch/` (decisions ledger, slice plan, scope trace, issues) are the tracker; never open the legacy checkout or follow `zenorm-legacy` references; product runs use their supplied records, not a parallel file tracker. A rejected operation remains a blocker with its proposed work retained.

Orient from the map index, then read the exact governing sources for the assigned question. Reuse full records already supplied. Retrieve omitted resolutions, relevant corrections, research and actual answers before relying on them. Older unrelated history stays retrievable. Keep each decision in its ticket, with a named link and one-line gist in the map. Keep vocabulary in the glossary.

Project documents govern their own project. Consult direction for decisions about that product, the glossary when its domain language matters, and testing agreements during synthesis or verification. A factual investigation into an external API needs its primary sources, not the host product's planning history.

## Questions and human authority

Save questions with the relevant work and revision using the supplied interaction tool. Retain their context, choices, recommendation and actual submitted choice/text answers. Requesting input ends the run; the app presents the round and resumes after every question has an answer. Reuse existing pending rounds. Silence, defaults and agent browser actions supply no human answer.

Text the human adds to a selected choice is commentary on that answer. It informs your reply and the resolution of that question; it is not map or specification content. Copy it into map notes, fog, exclusions or a specification only when a question explicitly decides it, or the human explicitly asks for the change. Notes about how the exercise is being run stay out of the plan.

Read submitted answers and recompute the frontier. Resolve Grilling when those answers settle the decision; ask follow-ups only for unresolved choices or ambiguity. Submission itself does not resolve a ticket or approve a contract. Reuse still-applicable agreements and ask about changed scope.

For Prototype/demo work, retain artifact identity, revision/variant, exercised surface, simulation limits, annotations and the actual scoped verdict. Selection, annotation resolution and final verdict remain distinct. A verdict confirms only its stated scope, not production correctness.

## Bounded assignments

Work the supplied assignment and claim. Each new decision, Research child, Prototype preparation and synthesis assignment gets fresh context; Grilling may resume its own ticket. The application schedules eligible work, including automatic Research/Prototype preparation, and presents progress. Independent discovery may run concurrently. Do not dispatch duplicate workers. A handoff names exact records, pending questions and the next bounded action.

For specification, ticket synthesis, reconciliation, implementation, review or handoff, read [contracts and delivery](zenorm-contracts.md). Those phases preserve exact contract revisions, review authority, completed evidence and verification obligations.
